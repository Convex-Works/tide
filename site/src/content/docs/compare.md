# Compare tide with Jitsi, Galène, MiroTalk, LiveKit Meet, Zoom and Google Meet

tide does less than the others. It is for teams that want their own meeting server and no more.

tide runs on LiveKit's media server, as LiveKit Meet does. LiveKit Meet is LiveKit's example app: it has no lobby, no host controls, no sign-in and no recording management. tide adds those.
